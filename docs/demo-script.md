## The 3 minutes (spoken script, adapt the words, keep the beats)

**0:00–0:30 — The problem, in shop language**
"Every bike shop I know loses the same hours: where's my order, what size am I,
will this part fit, my frame cracked. None of that needs your best mechanic or
your best salesperson — but all of it needs a *correct* answer. So I built an
agent that only answers from your catalogue, your orders, and your policies —
and drafts everything else for your approval."

**0:30–1:20 — Beat 1: Sizing + stock (run Test 1, then Test 2 live)**
Ask the sizing question on screen. Point at three things: it asked for inseam,
it named a price/stock from the catalogue, it flagged the out-of-stock size
instead of selling it. "A generic chatbot would have sold you the L. It's not in stock."

**1:20–2:00 — Beat 2: The traps (run Test 3, then Test 7 live)**
Do the Shimano-on-SRAM "just say yes" trap, then the cracked-frame demand.
"It won't confirm compatibility it can't source, and it won't promise a warranty
replacement. It collects the full packet — serial, proof, photos — in one go,
so your customer isn't in a 4-email thread. It proposes. You approve. Every time."

**2:00–2:40 — Beat 3: The owner report (show `08` output on `05`)**
"This is the part owners actually pay for. From 20 tickets it found: warranty
first replies averaging 40 hours, CSAT 2.2 — and the same bottle-boss crack on
the same model three times in nine days. That's a supplier conversation you
would have missed in an inbox."

**2:40–3:00 — The offer (no new prices — your live staircase)**
"It starts with my $595 Gorgias Helpdesk Audit on your real, anonymised tickets.
If it earns it, the build is my $1,800 Sprint, and care is $700 a month. You keep
your Gorgias and your data — I configure and tune. Link's below, or reply to this video."

## Objections — honest answers
- "ChatGPT can do this." → "ChatGPT will happily confirm a SRAM cassette fits —
  this agent refuses unless your own docs say so, and it drafts instead of sending."
- "Will it replace my staff?" → "No. It drafts and flags. Your staff approve —
  they just stop retyping WISMO replies."
- "What if the platform dies?" → "On Squidgy the agent skill exports as a file
  you keep, and the real setup lives in your Gorgias, which is yours regardless."
- "What's it cost me in AI fees?" → "You pay your own Gorgias/platform bills
  directly — Gorgias AI is roughly $0.90 per fully resolved conversation on top
  of your plan, in writing before we launch. I never mark up your credits."
- "My data?" → "We start on exported, anonymised tickets. No live helpdesk
  access until you say so, in writing."
